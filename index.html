Who We Are

The professional home of Ghana's health accounting staff

From regional and teaching hospitals to district clinics and national health agencies, HASAG sets the professional standard for how public health resources are recorded, reported and safeguarded — one national body, one shared code of practice.

  • One national body with sixteen regional chapters and a single code of ethics.
  • A welfare and benevolence scheme that stands with every member and their family.
  • Structured continuous professional development, workshops and national congresses.

Our Code

Four principles govern every ledger we keep

Our members are custodians of public trust. These values are not aspirations — they are conditions of membership.

01

Integrity

Honest records without exception. Our name and our conduct must always agree — that is the meaning of Hasag ·· Integrity.

02

Accountability

Every cedi of public health money must answer to a name, a voucher and a verifiable trail.

03

Transparency

Open books, clear reporting and honest disclosure — from district clinic to national agency.

04

Excellence

Continuous learning, professional certification and mastery of modern public financial management.

Our Mission

To organise, train and protect the interests of health accounting staff across Ghana — while holding every member to the highest standard of financial integrity.

Our Vision

A Ghanaian health sector where every cedi of public money is recorded, reported and safeguarded by a qualified, respected and united accounting profession.

Our Story

Thirty-eight years of organised service

From a handful of hospital accounts clerks to a chartered national union — the milestones that shaped HASAG.

1988

Founding of the Union

Accounts staff from a handful of regional hospitals convene in Accra to form a professional body dedicated to standards, welfare and training — HASAG is born.

1994

Constitution Ratified

Delegates adopt HASAG's founding constitution at the first National Delegates Congress, establishing the National Executive Council and the Council of Elders.

2003

Welfare & Benevolence Fund Launched

A dues-funded welfare scheme begins supporting members and their families through bereavement, illness and hardship — still a cornerstone of membership today.

2015

National Coverage Completed

The sixteenth and final regional chapter is chartered, giving HASAG an unbroken presence across every region of Ghana.

2022

GIFMIS & IFRS Rollout

HASAG partners with the Ministry of Health to deliver nationwide GIFMIS and IFRS-aligned reporting training, digitising how facility accounts are kept.

2025 — Present

Kumasi Congress & Digital Secretariat

A new National Executive is elected at the Kumasi Congress, ushering in digitised dues administration and this online home for the Union.

How We're Governed

A structure built on accountability

HASAG's constitution separates leadership, oversight and wisdom into three distinct bodies — so no single office holds unchecked authority over the Union's affairs.

Meet the National Executive

National Executive Council

Nine elected officers who run the Union's day-to-day affairs and represent it nationally.

Council of Elders

Founding and senior members who guide governance, discipline and constitutional interpretation.

Patrons

Distinguished non-members who lend their standing and counsel to the Union's mission.

Regional Chapters

Sixteen chapters, one per region, each electing its own officers and running local welfare and training.
